# Reviewer Readiness

## Manuscript Status

The manuscript is a conditional mathematical foundations proposal. Its exact
results are confined to declared occupied operator systems and explicit source
packets.

## Proven Internally

- A complete record edge induces a faithful trace-preserving standard-form
  isometry under the stated hypotheses.
- Finite recovery completeness plus primitive extensivity forces the exact
  Trace--Gram response on the admissible chord domain.
- Path composition preserves the identity, while descriptor-visible holonomy
  must remain physical path data.
- Exact CPTP recovery preserves fidelity and the Uhlmann chord on the recovered
  operator system.
- A uniform trace-norm recovery error, and therefore a full-code diamond-norm
  certificate, gives an explicit finite Uhlmann-chord loss bound.
- The three-qubit bit-flip toy satisfies the exact recovery identity.
- Commuting record statistics cannot identify reversibility.

## Not Proven

- Physical creation or occupation of complete edges.
- Unrestricted parent-law realization and physical occupation of the special
  dagger-Frobenius normalization, represented
  central trace weights, exact extensivity, or finite recovery completeness.
- A laboratory Tau carrier or a passed FOC-7 certificate.
- A physically identified recovery map and experimental error budget
  sufficient for a specific carrier.

## Reviewer Attack Surface

The strongest assumptions are finite recovery completeness, exact primitive
extensivity, the common action unit, and physical occupation. The manuscript
states each as an assumption or test target rather than a theorem of
unrestricted physical realization.
